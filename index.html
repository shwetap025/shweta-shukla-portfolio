Curriculum redesignHealthcareChange management

Navigator role curriculum redesign

A decision-centered curriculum for 200+ healthcare navigators who needed to resolve complex cases with greater confidence and consistency.

35%fewer supervisor escalations

28%faster case resolution

85%correctly applied escalation criteria

Read the project story

The challenge. Training inherited from an adjacent customer-service role emphasized information lookup instead of judgment. Navigators lacked clear criteria for when to resolve a case and when to escalate it.

My approach. I led an eight-person team through role analysis, SME validation, and scenario design. We organized six modules around real workflows, created 12 branching scenarios from operational error logs, and developed job aids for live calls.

The result. The new curriculum reduced supervisor dependence, improved case speed, and raised correct application of escalation criteria from a 52% baseline to 85%.
